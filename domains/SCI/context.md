# SCI — Science & Health

## Scope
Pandemics, clinical trials, space exploration, medical breakthroughs, Nobel prizes, physics discoveries.

## Mental Models
- **Base rates for breakthroughs** — most promising research fails; expect long timelines
- **Clinical trial phases** — Phase 1/2/3 have different success rates (~10% drug approval overall)
- **Replication crisis** — many published findings don't replicate; weight meta-analyses
- **Technology readiness levels** — lab demo ≠ commercial product
- **Pandemic preparedness** — next pandemic is when, not if
