# TEC — Recommended Strategies

*The insider's domain. AI/tech domain knowledge creates genuine calibration edge.*

## Why TEC Has Opportunity

- **Moderate participant sophistication.** Tech enthusiasts and some insiders, but fewer systematic traders than POL or ECO.
- **Following AI research closely (arxiv, benchmarks, industry gossip) creates real edge.** Most market participants trade on headline announcements, not research trends.
- **Growing liquidity.** AI markets are attracting more attention on Polymarket as AI becomes the dominant tech narrative.
- **Resolution clarity is the main risk.** "Will GPT-5 launch by [date]?" depends on what counts as "GPT-5" vs a renamed model.

## Strategy Priority (ordered)

### 1. S001 Hold to Resolution — **PRIMARY** (Score: 4/5)

Calibration edge from deep AI/tech knowledge.

**Why it works here**: If you follow arxiv papers, benchmark leaks, product timelines, and industry hiring patterns, you know more about model capabilities and release timelines than the average Polymarket participant. This translates directly to calibration edge on markets like "Claude 4 by [date]" or "GPT-5 by [date]."

**How to deploy**:
- Form probability estimate from research BEFORE checking market
- Enter if divergence ≥10%
- Hold to resolution (test calibration)
- Track Brier score in TEC specifically

### 2. S008 Information Edge — **PRIMARY** (Score: 4/5)

Research effort barrier is real. Arxiv synthesis, benchmark analysis, industry insider knowledge.

**Why it works here**: Type A (synthesis) — combining arxiv papers + industry hiring + compute buildout data. Type B (base rate) — how often do announced product timelines hold? Type E (attention) — stale markets that haven't moved despite new benchmark results.

**How to deploy**:
- For each TEC market: check recent arxiv papers, product announcements, benchmark results
- Classify edge type
- Enter on divergence, hold to resolution unless consensus catches up
- Document in `TEC/research/`

### 3. S003 News Catalyst — **SECONDARY** (Score: 4/5)

Product launches and tech conferences are scheduled catalysts.

**Why it works here**: WWDC, GTC, Google I/O, product launch events — all on the calendar. Markets on "will Apple announce X?" or "GPT-5 demo at [conference]?" create catalyst opportunities.

**How to deploy**:
- Build TEC catalyst calendar (major conferences, product launch windows)
- Enter 3-7 days before catalyst
- Assess: is the market pricing in the announcement? If "GPT-5 by conference" is already at 80%, the catalyst is priced.

## Strategies to Skip in TEC

- **S002 Threshold (2/5)**: Low volatility. Gradual price discovery means thresholds rarely trigger.
- **S004 Contrarian (3/5)**: Some AI hype overreaction, but less dramatic than GEO/CRY.
- **S005 Momentum (3/5)**: AI model leapfrogging creates narratives, but timescale is long and resolution fuzzy.
- **S006 Time Decay (3/5)**: "GPT-5 by [date]" markets exist but resolution clarity risk makes the math less clean than GEO.
