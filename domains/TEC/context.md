# TEC — Technology & AI

## Scope
AI capabilities and benchmarks, product launches, tech regulation, compute scaling, company strategy, AI safety, AGI timelines.

## Mental Models
- **Scaling laws** — more compute/data → better models, but three axes now: pre-training, post-training, test-time compute
- **S-curves** — technologies follow adoption S-curves; AI coding agents are in steep growth phase
- **Platform shifts** — mobile → cloud → AI agents. The agent era is here.
- **Regulatory lag** — EU AI Act enforcement ramps Aug 2026; US under Trump favors innovation-first
- **Overton window** — what's considered possible shifts; yesterday's science fiction is today's product
- **Benchmark saturation** — MMLU, HumanEval, MATH essentially solved. Frontier moved to ARC-AGI-2, HLE, SWE-bench
- **Deployment vs capability** — having the tech ≠ deploying it at scale
- **Arena dynamics** — Chatbot Arena rankings have become THE resolution criterion for "best model" markets. Style control ON vs OFF matters. New models need weeks to accumulate votes.
