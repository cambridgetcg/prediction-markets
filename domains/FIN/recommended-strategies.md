# FIN — Recommended Strategies

*TradFi spillover zone. Edge is rare — play the structure, not the knowledge game.*

## Why FIN Is Tough

- **Highest participant sophistication.** TradFi pros, quants, and algo traders dominate. Any research edge is priced in traditional markets first, then bleeds into prediction markets.
- **Correlated with efficient financial markets.** Prediction market prices on "will NVDA be largest company?" track the underlying stock price. You're competing with the NYSE.
- **Lower liquidity on Polymarket** ($961K on largest company market) compared to underlying markets. Thin markets + sophisticated participants = hard terrain.
- **Any information edge you have is already available in options, futures, and equities.** The prediction market is a lagging mirror.

## Strategy Priority (ordered)

### 1. S003 News Catalyst — **PRIMARY** (Score: 4/5)

Earnings and product launches create predictable catalysts.

**Why it works here**: While the market is efficient on aggregate, specific events can create temporary mispricings. Earnings surprises, FDA approvals for pharma, product launch announcements — these are scheduled and their outcomes create asymmetric price moves.

**How to deploy**:
- Build a FIN catalyst calendar (major earnings, product launches, regulatory decisions)
- Identify prediction markets affected by each catalyst
- Enter 3-7 days before if the market hasn't fully priced the catalyst
- Conservative sizing (3% max) — you're fighting TradFi on their home turf

### 2. S002 Threshold Exit — **SECONDARY** (Score: 3/5)

Underlying market volatility creates tradable swings.

**Why it works here**: When the S&P drops 3% in a day, prediction markets on related topics (recession probability, tech sector dominance) swing. These swings often revert. S002 thresholds can capture the volatility.

**How to deploy**:
- Enter on FIN prediction markets that swing with underlying market moves
- S002b thresholds (1.5x profit / -40% stop)
- Pair with S004 if the underlying move was emotional (e.g., VIX spike on headline)

### 3. S005 Momentum — **SELECTIVE** (Score: 3/5)

Equity trend correlation creates some momentum opportunity.

**Why it works here**: If NVDA is on a multi-week rally, "NVDA largest company" markets follow. The prediction market may lag the underlying.

**How to deploy**:
- Only when underlying equity trend is confirmed by multiple indicators
- Enter on 3-5% pullback within the prediction market trend
- Trailing 15% stop

## Strategies to Avoid in FIN

- **S001 Hold (2/5)**: TradFi pros out-calibrate you. Your independent estimate adds nothing.
- **S004 Contrarian (2/5)**: Efficient markets recover too quickly or the moves are justified.
- **S006 Time Decay (2/5)**: Less "by date" structure. Price-target markets track underlying, not time premium.
- **S008 Info Edge (2/5)**: Any research edge available to you is already priced in the underlying. Don't bring a knife to a gunfight.

## The FIN Rule

**If you can trade the underlying equity/option more efficiently, do that instead.** FIN prediction markets are a second-order derivative. The edge, if it exists, is in structural features (S003 catalysts, S002 volatility capture), not in knowing the direction better.
