# FIN — Financial Markets

## Scope
Equities, indices, commodities, corporate events (earnings, M&A, IPOs), market cap rankings.

## Mental Models
- **Efficient market hypothesis** — prices reflect available info (mostly)
- **Momentum** — trends persist more than random walk suggests
- **Mean reversion** — extremes tend to correct
- **Earnings growth** — long-term stock returns track earnings growth
- **Valuation multiples** — P/E expansion/contraction drives medium-term returns
- **Concentration risk** — when a few stocks dominate indices

## Key Context (2026)
- AI/tech concentration: NVIDIA leading market cap race
- Magnificent 7 dynamics
- Commodity supercycle narrative vs demand uncertainty
