# SOC — Recommended Strategies

*Fun money zone. Trade the events calendar, don't pretend to have an edge on pop culture.*

## Why SOC Is Low Priority

- **Hard to have systematic calibration edge on pop culture.** Whimsical outcomes driven by taste, fashion, and unpredictable human behavior.
- **Casual participants.** Entertainment fans, not analysts. Low sophistication creates some opportunity but also unpredictable pricing.
- **Moderate liquidity** for big events (GTA VI, Eurovision, Oscars). Very thin for niche cultural markets.
- **Moderate volatility** on rumors and announcements. Entertainment hype cycles exist.

## Strategy Priority (ordered)

### 1. S003 News Catalyst — **PRIMARY** (Score: 4/5)

Entertainment event calendar is the best angle.

**Why it works here**: Game releases, award ceremonies, sporting events, TV premieres — all scheduled. Markets on "GTA VI release by [date]" or "Eurovision winner" have clear catalysts. Industry knowledge (gaming leaks, award season dynamics) creates slight edge.

**How to deploy**:
- Build SOC catalyst calendar (major releases, award shows, competitions)
- Enter 3-7 days before catalyst if market misprices the outcome
- Exit on event day
- Treat as fun money — 2-3% max position size

### 2. S002 Threshold Exit — **SECONDARY** (Score: 3/5)

Rumor-driven swings create some threshold opportunity.

**Why it works here**: "GTA VI delayed" rumors, celebrity drama, trailer drops — these cause moderate price swings. S002 thresholds capture the volatility.

**How to deploy**:
- Standard S002b thresholds
- Only on markets with sufficient liquidity
- Momentum override on announcement-driven moves

## Strategies to Skip in SOC

- **S001 Hold (2/5)**: No systematic calibration edge on pop culture.
- **S008 Info Edge (2/5)**: Pop culture research doesn't scale. Edge is fleeting.
- **S006 Time Decay (2/5)**: "By date" structures exist but outcomes aren't fear-driven. No systematic time premium overpricing.
- **S007 Arb (2/5)**: Mostly standalone markets. No connected outcomes.
- **S004 Contrarian (3/5)**: Some overreaction to entertainment news, but hard to systematize.
- **S005 Momentum (3/5)**: Cultural hype cycles exist but outcomes are discrete events.

## The SOC Rule

**Treat SOC as calibration entertainment.** Small positions, event-driven timing, no serious capital allocation. If you find yourself doing deep research on Eurovision odds, reconsider your time allocation.
