## 2. France

### Electoral System

- **Two-round direct vote**: First round (all candidates), top 2 advance to runoff
- **Front republicain**: Historical practice of cross-party consolidation against far-right in round 2
  - 2002: Chirac 82% vs Le Pen 18% — massive barrage
  - 2017: Macron 66% vs Le Pen 34% — declining
  - 2022: Macron 59% vs Le Pen 41% — further erosion
  - **Trend**: If barrage drops below ~55%, RN wins
- **Term limits**: 2 consecutive terms. Macron cannot run in 2027.
- **Next election**: April 2027 (first round April 8-23, runoff 2 weeks later)

**PM Sebastien Lecornu** (4th PM in 2 years) governs without majority. Survived 8 no-confidence votes. Passed 2026 budget on Feb 2 by suspending Macron's pension reform.

### Marine Le Pen — The July 7 Catalyst

**Conviction (March 31, 2025)**: Found guilty of embezzling EU Parliament funds. Sentence:
- 4 years imprisonment (2 suspended, 2 house arrest)
- 100K EUR fine
- **5-year ban from office — with immediate provisional enforcement**

**Appeal timeline**:
- Appeal trial: Jan 13 — Feb 12, 2026
- Prosecutor recommended 5-year ban **without** immediate enforcement (softer stance)
- **Ruling: July 7, 2026** — THE key date

**Scenarios**:
1. Ban upheld with immediate enforcement → Le Pen definitively out of 2027, Bardella consolidates RN
2. Ban upheld without immediate enforcement → Le Pen could technically run while further appeals proceed
3. Conviction overturned → Le Pen runs as frontrunner
