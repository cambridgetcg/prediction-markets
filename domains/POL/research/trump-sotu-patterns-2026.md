### Signature Trump Words
- **"great"** is anomalously high for any president. Used as universal amplifier: "great, great wall," "our great Republican tax cuts," "a great education"
- **"very"** — Trump uses this ~2x more than any other president in SOTU history
- **"beautiful"** — frequent in off-script remarks, less common in formal SOTU text
- **"tremendous"** — more common in rallies/pressers than formal SOTU text

### Words Trump AVOIDS in SOTUs
- **"democracy"** — did NOT appear in 2017, 2018, or 2019 SOTUs. First appeared in 2020 (discussing Venezuela). Biden used it 42 times across his SOTUs.
- **"inflation"** — did NOT appear in any 1st-term SOTU (2017-2020), despite discussing economy extensively

### 2026 SOTU Specific Word Frequencies
- **"border" and "tax"**: tied as most repeated at 16 mentions each

- **Immigration language**: "illegal alien" appeared prominently
- **Economic terms**: "inflation," "jobs," "economy," "stock market" all frequent
- **Affirmative language**: "winning" and "better" used repeatedly

## 3. Rhetorical Patterns & Style

### Signature Devices (Academic Research)
1. **Hyperbole**: "the greatest," "never before," "everyone," "the best in history" — superlatives and maximal quantifiers are core strategy
2. **Repetition**: Anaphora ("We will... We will... We will...") and epiphora ("...again" repeated)
3. **Simple vocabulary**: Speaks at 4th-grade reading level (lowest of last 15 presidents per Newsweek analysis)
4. **Emotional appeals**: Targets voter insecurity, promises restoration of past "greatness"
5. **Binary framing**: Things are "terrible" or "incredible," "best" or "worst" — no middle ground
6. **Intensifier stacking**: "very, very," "many, many," rare "super-duper"

### Catchphrases by Context
- **SOTU-specific**: "State of our Union is strong" (formal), "golden age" (2026 theme)
- **Cross-context**: "MAGA," "fake news," "radical left," "sleepy Joe"
- **Self-reference**: Frequently refers to himself in third person ("Trump")

## 7. Key Lessons for Future SOTU Prediction Markets

### Reliable Bets (historically high-probability)
1. **Speech over 80 minutes**: 100% hit rate in 2nd term. Bet OVER on length.
2. **"America/American" 25+ times**: Near-certainty for any Trump SOTU
3. **Iran mentioned**: 100% across all Trump SOTUs
4. **"Great" used multiple times**: Signature word, always present
5. **Immigration/border content**: Always a major topic
6. **Self-reference ("Trump")**: Very high probability
7. **Guest introductions**: Always includes emotional storytelling with named guests

### Traps / Overpriced Markets
1. **China mentions**: Despite pre-speech odds of 93%, Trump BARELY mentioned China in 2026. Same pattern in 2018 (1 mention). Strategic omissions are real.
2. **"Fake news"**: Has NEVER appeared in a formal SOTU. Rally language ≠ SOTU language.
3. **Specific nicknames (Sleepy Joe)**: Less common in formal SOTU context vs. rallies
4. **DOGE/Musk**: Programs/people that have become politically complicated get omitted
5. **North Korea**: Not mentioned at all in 2026 despite being major in 1st term

### Edge Sources
1. **Guest list leaks**: Knowing who's attending reveals which topics get human-interest segments (Hockey team → hockey mention, Sage Blair → transgender mention)
2. **Pre-speech Trump comments**: Trump told reporters it would be "a long speech" — shifted market from 40% to 70% for 100+ min
3. **Diplomatic calendar**: China trip scheduled for late March → China omission predictable
4. **Press secretary briefings**: Named "Operation Midnight Hammer" before speech
5. **White House fact sheets**: DOI published "Golden Age" framing beforehand
6. **70% YES resolution rate**: General bias toward YES in mention markets — but specific "rally words" (fake news, nicknames) are overpriced

### Trump's SOTU vs. Rally Language (Critical Distinction)
The formal SOTU is NOT a rally. Key differences:
- **Less**: "fake news," nicknames, spontaneous tangents, crowd banter
- **More**: "American," "America," policy specifics, guest stories, medal ceremonies
- **Same**: "great," "beautiful," immigration, self-congratulation, superlatives
- **SOTU-specific**: "State of our Union is strong," "golden age," formal policy framing
