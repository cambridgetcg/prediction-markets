## 1. Bitcoin On-Chain

### Hash Rate
- **Current**: ~1.136 ZH/s (zettahash), recovered from storm-induced dip to 826 EH/s
- **Difficulty**: 144.4T — jumped 15%, largest increase since 2021
- **Trend**: Structural uptrend despite price weakness. Winter storm in the US temporarily forced several major miners offline, but hashrate has fully recovered.
- **Signal**: Miners are investing in capacity despite operating at a loss, suggesting long-term conviction. However, centralization risk was exposed during the storm.
- Sources: [CoinWarz](https://www.coinwarz.com/mining/bitcoin/hashrate-chart), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/02/20/bitcoin-difficulty-jumps-15-largest-increase-since-2021-despite-price-slump), [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/hashrate-index-roundup-february-16-2026/)

### Active Addresses
- **Current**: ~700,000-1,000,000 daily active addresses (300,000-500,000 unique users)
- **Trend**: Relatively flat. In October 2024, daily active addresses dropped to ~650K despite rising price, indicating HODLing behavior. The metric has not significantly recovered.
- **Signal**: Low active addresses with low price = accumulation phase. On-chain activity is subdued, suggesting the market is in a wait-and-see mode.
- Sources: [Bitcoin Magazine Pro](https://www.bitcoinmagazinepro.com/charts/bitcoin-active-addresses/), [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com/asset/btc/chart/addresses/active-addresses)

### Exchange Balances
- **Current**: 2.4M-2.8M BTC on centralized exchanges — multi-year lows
- **Trend**: Structural downtrend for 4+ years. Net outflows surged 130% in late Dec/early Jan (16,563 BTC -> 38,500 BTC in net outflows)
- **Signal**: STRONGLY BULLISH for supply dynamics. Coins are moving to cold storage/self-custody. Thinner order book liquidity means even small demand spikes (ETF purchase, sovereign buyer) could trigger explosive price action.
- Sources: [Blockchain Reporter](https://blockchainreporter.net/bitcoin-exchange-balances-hit-multi-year-lows-what-20000-btc-exodus-signals-for-2026/), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/381341/crypto-markets-quiet-strength-bitcoin-exchange-balances), [CoinGlass](https://www.coinglass.com/Balance), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/Opinion/26/01/49812023/bitcoin-exchange-balances-are-falling-as-market-structure-evolves)

### MVRV Ratio
- **Current**: ~1.1-1.2 (approaching undervalued territory)
- **365-day MVRV**: -11% (market price 11% below average holder cost as of Feb 12)
- **Historical context**: Below 1 = deep undervaluation/capitulation. Above 3.7 = euphoria/top. Current level (1.1-1.2) is approaching historically significant buy zones.
- **ETF MVRV**: Has slipped below 1.0, meaning ETF holders are underwater on average.
- **Signal**: BULLISH from a contrarian perspective. The ratio suggests BTC is trading near or slightly above realized value — historically a zone where bottoms form.
- Sources: [CryptBull](https://www.cryptbull.net/2026/02/21/bitcoin-trades-below-etf-cost-basis-as-mvrv-signals-mounting-pressure/), [CryptoNewsZ](https://www.cryptonewsz.com/bitcoin-price-mvrv-historic-reversal-zone/), [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com/asset/btc/chart/market-indicator/mvrv-ratio)

### Long-Term Holder vs Short-Term Holder Behavior

- **STH (<155 days)**: Significantly reduced positions. STH Realized Price sits at ~$113,000, acting as overhead resistance.
- **Signal**: BULLISH inflection. LTH accumulation historically precedes major rallies. The fact that LTHs are buying at $67K while STH cost basis is $113K suggests conviction among experienced holders.
- Sources: [AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/bitcoin-long-term-holder-dynamics-critical-inflection-point-2026-2601/), [TradingView/NewsBTC](https://www.tradingview.com/news/newsbtc:69509f3c8094b:0-bitcoin-long-term-holder-dump-is-over-on-chain-data-just-flipped/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bitcoin-long-term-holders-accumulate-124336384.html)

- Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/02/05/bitcoin-trades-20-below-its-production-cost-as-miner-stress-intensifies), [AInvest/Cango](https://www.ainvest.com/news/cango-sells-4-451-btc-miner-selling-adds-pressure-bitcoin-2026-2602-42/), [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/cango-sells-4451-btc-as-miner-selling-adds-pressure-to-bitcoin-in-2026/)

## 2. Ethereum On-Chain

### Total ETH Staked & Staking Ratio
- **Total staked**: ~37.25M ETH (~$120B at current prices)
- **Staking ratio**: 30.8% of total supply — crossed 30% for first time in Feb 2026
- **Growth rate**: From 29.3% at end of 2025 to 30.8% in 6 weeks
- **Staking yield**: 3.5%-4.2% APY
- **Signal**: Bullish for network security. However, controversy exists — Santiment's claim of 50% staking was criticized as misleading by researchers.
- Sources: [CoinLaw](https://coinlaw.io/eth-staking-statistics/), [HokaNews](https://www.hokanews.com/2026/02/ethereum-hits-historic-milestone-as-30.html), [ChainLabo](https://www.chainlabo.com/blog/ethereum-staking-rate-30-percent-2026-security-settlement-layer), [DataWallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/ethereum-staking-statistics-and-trends)

### L2 Activity (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base)
- **Total L2 daily transactions**: ~2M (roughly 2x Ethereum mainnet volume)
- **Market share**: Base (46.6%), Arbitrum (30.9%), Optimism — collectively ~90% of all L2 transactions
- **Base**: 11.57M transactions/24h, 663K active addresses/24h — operating at consumer scale
- **Arbitrum TVL**: ~$18B (dominant in DeFi), ~31% of L2 DeFi TVL
- **Consolidation trend**: Smaller L2s becoming "zombie chains" with usage dropping 61%
- **Projection**: L2 TVL expected to exceed L1 DeFi TVL by Q3 2026 ($150B vs $130B)
- **Signal**: L2 adoption is strong and accelerating, but heavily concentrated. Base is the clear winner. This is bullish for Ethereum as a settlement layer but raises questions about ETH value accrual.
- Sources: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/383329/2026-layer-2-outlook), [CryptoRank/21Shares](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/86026-most-ethereum-l2s-may-not-survive-2026-as-base-arbitrum-optimism-tighten-grip-21shares), [Coin Bureau](https://coinbureau.com/analysis/what-is-the-best-layer-2), [L2BEAT](https://l2beat.com/scaling/tvs)

### Blob Fee Revenue
- **Cumulative blob fees since Dencun**: ~$26M (modest)
- **2025 H1 blob spend**: ~1,900 ETH (~$5.4M), about 1.5% of Ethereum's total Real Economic Value
- **Recent upgrade**: Blob target raised to ~14 per block (max 21) on Jan 7, 2026
- **Upcoming**: Fusaka upgrade could double or triple blob capacity within weeks of going live
- **Missing revenue estimate**: Had EIP-7918 been active since Dencun, an additional ~$78.6M would have been generated
- **Signal**: Blob fees remain negligible today but are positioned as Ethereum's key long-term revenue source. Justin Drake calls them "the only sustainable source of value flow" for ETH.
- Sources: [Fidelity Digital Assets](https://www.fidelitydigitalassets.com/research-and-insights/fusaka-upgrade-scaling-meets-value-accrual), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/01/08/ethereum-bumps-blob-capacity-as-it-gears-for-fusaka-upgrade), [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/ethereum-blobs-balancing-act), [Blobscan](https://blobscan.com/)

### DeFi TVL
- **Ethereum L1 DeFi TVL**: ~$70B (68% of all DeFi TVL across chains)
- **Trend**: Stable to growing. Institutional adoption and tokenized assets are the new growth drivers.
- **Growth catalysts**: Stablecoin market projected to reach $500B, tokenized assets projected at $300B by 2026
- **Upcoming**: Glamsterdam fork (mid-2026) increases gas limit from 60M to 200M per block, enabling ~10K TPS on L1
- Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ethereum-tvl-could-surge-10-084700894.html), [AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/ethereum-tenfold-tvl-growth-2026-strategic-play-institutional-adoption-tokenized-assets-2512/)

### ETH Supply Dynamics
- **Current state**: Mildly INFLATIONARY at ~0.24% annual inflation (down from 0.26%)
- **Cause**: Dencun upgrade (March 2024) reduced L2 fees paid to L1, cutting burn rate
- **Trend**: Inflation trending lower; analysts project potential return to deflationary by mid-2026 if network activity picks up
- **Context**: Despite burning $18B in ETH since EIP-1559, supply has been growing since Dencun shifted L2 economics
- **Signal**: NEUTRAL to slightly bearish. The "ultrasound money" narrative is weakened. Fusaka/Glamsterdam upgrades may restore deflationary dynamics, but this is speculative.
- Sources: [CoinLedger](https://coinledger.io/learn/ultrasound-money), [21Shares](https://www.21shares.com/en-eu/research/ethereum-2026-outlook-staked-slightly-inflationary-levered-by-scalability), [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/ethereum-longest-inflationary-period-since-merge)

### Ethereum Summary
| Metric | Value | Signal |
|--------|-------|--------|
| ETH staked | 37.25M (30.8%) | Bullish (security, supply lock) |
| L2 activity | ~2M daily txs, Base dominant | Bullish (adoption), Neutral (ETH value accrual) |
| Blob fees | ~$26M cumulative | Neutral (early, growing) |
| DeFi TVL | ~$70B (68% market share) | Bullish (dominance) |
| Supply dynamics | +0.24% inflation | Slightly bearish (narrative risk) |

**Overall ETH Assessment**: Ethereum's fundamentals are solid but the value accrual question remains. L2 adoption is strong but concentrating, and the network is mildly inflationary. The staking milestone and upcoming Fusaka/Glamsterdam upgrades are positive catalysts. ETH's investment thesis now hinges more on institutional adoption and tokenized assets than on the "ultrasound money" deflationary narrative.

## 3. Solana On-Chain

### TPS and Network Usage
- **Current real-world TPS**: 1,000-5,000 TPS during normal operation, spikes above 100K in stress tests
- **Theoretical ceiling**: 65,000 TPS
- **Daily active wallets**: ~3M
- **Upcoming**: Firedancer client handled 1M TPS in testing; full mainnet deployment expected to eliminate outages and reduce latency to sub-150ms
- **Signal**: Solana continues to deliver the highest real-world throughput among major L1s. Firedancer is a potentially transformative upgrade.
- Sources: [StealthEX](https://stealthex.io/blog/solana-blockchain/), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/11/2-game-changing-updates-coming-to-solana-in-2026/), [Chainspect](https://chainspect.app/chain/solana), [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/statistics)

### Developer Activity
- **2024**: 7,625 new developers (largest single-year onboarding for any chain)
- **2025 (first 9 months)**: 11,534 new developers added (second only to Ethereum)
- **Growth**: ~78% increase in developer interest over last 2 years
- **2026 focus areas**: Reduced developer costs, expanded transaction scale, optimized on-chain programs, lower storage costs (per Anza's roadmap)
- **Signal**: BULLISH. Sustained developer growth is the strongest leading indicator for ecosystem health. Solana trails only Ethereum.
- Sources: [MEXC News](https://www.mexc.co/en-PH/news/365908), [Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/anza-outlines-key-developments-for-solana-in-2026-54094), [CoinLaw](https://coinlaw.io/blockchain-developer-activity-statistics/)

### DeFi TVL
- **Current**: ~$9-10B (down from ATH of $12.2B in Sept 2025)
- **Position**: Second-largest DeFi ecosystem after Ethereum
- **RWA TVL**: $873M (400% growth)
- **DEX volume**: >$1.5T cumulative
- **2025 app revenue**: $2.39B (46% YoY increase)
- **Signal**: TVL has pulled back from peaks but remains substantial. DEX activity and app revenue show real economic activity, not just speculative flows.
- Sources: [BlockEden Forum](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/solanas-rwa-tvl-hit-873m-with-400-growth-and-dex-volume-topped-1-5-trillion-but-meme-coin-revenue-still-drives-the-ecosystem/498), [MEXC](https://www.mexc.co/en-NG/news/90964), [Disruption Banking](https://www.disruptionbanking.com/2026/01/20/how-strong-will-solana-be-in-2026/)

### Meme Coin Dynamics (Pump.fun)
- **Status**: Still VERY active. Pump.fun hit ATH DEX volume exceeding $2B
- **Platform stats**: 11.9M tokens created since Jan 2024, >$800M cumulative revenue
- **Market share**: Up to 71% of all daily token launches on Solana
- **PUMP token**: 60% price rally in early 2026, driven by strategic buybacks
- **Revenue model**: Transaction fees on meme coin trading
- **Signal**: MIXED. Meme coin activity drives fees and economic activity but raises sustainability concerns. If meme coin mania fades, a significant portion of Solana's revenue base could evaporate.
- Sources: [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/pump-funs-dex-volume-hits-a-new-ath/), [Yahoo Finance/Pump.fun](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/pump-fun-dex-volume-hits-132415661.html), [Phemex](https://phemex.com/academy/what-is-pump-fun-solana-meme-coin-launchpad-pump-token)

### Network Reliability
- **Recent status**: No outages reported in 2026 (as of Feb 23)
- **Last officially acknowledged outage**: February 6, 2024
- **Unofficial disruptions**: StatusGator detected at least 9 disruptions from Oct 2024-Feb 2025, some lasting ~13 hours, none officially reported
- **Historical**: 7 separate outage incidents over past 5 years (5 from client bugs, 2 from transaction spam)
- **Design philosophy**: Solana prioritizes safety over liveness — halts rather than risks state corruption
- **Signal**: Improving but history of unreported disruptions is concerning. Firedancer is expected to significantly improve reliability.
- Sources: [Solana Status](https://status.solana.com/), [Helius](https://www.helius.dev/blog/solana-outages-complete-history), [StatusGator](https://statusgator.com/blog/solana-outage-history/)

### Solana Summary
| Metric | Value | Signal |
|--------|-------|--------|
| TPS | 1,000-5,000 real-world | Bullish (highest L1 throughput) |
| Developer activity | 11,534 new devs in 2025 | Bullish (strong growth) |
| DeFi TVL | ~$9-10B | Neutral (down from ATH but #2) |
| Meme coin/Pump.fun | ATH volume, $2B+ DEX vol | Mixed (revenue driver but risky) |
| Network reliability | No 2026 outages (officially) | Improving (Firedancer will help) |

**Overall SOL Assessment**: Solana's fundamentals are strong in usage metrics (TPS, developers, DEX volume) but the meme coin dependency is a structural concern. The Firedancer upgrade could be transformative for both performance and reliability. TVL has pulled back from peaks, tracking with broader market weakness. The RWA growth (+400%) suggests diversification beyond meme coins.

## 5. Key Takeaways for Prediction Markets

### Macro On-Chain Picture
The on-chain data across all three chains paints a picture of a market in a **corrective/accumulation phase** with improving long-term fundamentals:

1. **BTC is showing classic cycle-bottom signals**: MVRV near 1.0, miner capitulation, LTH accumulation, exchange balances at multi-year lows. These have historically preceded major rallies, though timing remains uncertain.

2. **ETH is at an inflection point**: Strong staking and L2 adoption, but the value accrual question (inflationary supply, reduced fee burns) creates narrative headwinds. Fusaka and Glamsterdam upgrades in 2026 could resolve this.

3. **SOL has the strongest usage metrics** but faces concentration risk (meme coin dependency). Developer growth and Firedancer upgrade are strong long-term positives.

### Prediction Market Angles
- **"Will BTC reach $X by Y date?"** — On-chain data suggests favorable risk/reward for bullish positions if MVRV remains near 1.0 and LTH accumulation continues. But $87K production cost means miner selling pressure persists until price clears that level.
- **"Will ETH return to deflationary?"** — Likely by mid-2026 if Fusaka delivers and network activity picks up. High-probability YES at reasonable odds.
- **"Will Solana experience a major outage in 2026?"** — History suggests possible but improving. Firedancer rollout is the key variable.
- **"Will Base surpass Arbitrum in DeFi TVL?"** — Already leading in transactions; TVL convergence seems likely. Watch for this market.
