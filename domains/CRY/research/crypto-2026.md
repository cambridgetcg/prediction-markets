# Crypto Market Research — February 23, 2026

## Executive Summary

The crypto market is in a severe correction. Bitcoin is down 49% from its October 2025 ATH of $126,198, trading at ~$64,800. The selloff is driven by Trump tariff uncertainty, massive ETF outflows ($4.5B in 8 weeks), a hawkish Fed pause, and rotation into gold. Fear & Greed Index hit 5 — an all-time record low, worse than FTX collapse. However, on-chain metrics show classic bottoming signals (MVRV ~1.1, miner capitulation, LTH accumulation, exchange outflows at multi-year lows), and the regulatory environment is the most favorable in US history.

## 2. Halving Cycle Position

**Last halving: April 20, 2024. Currently ~22 months post-halving.**

| Cycle | Halving | Peak | Months to Peak | Peak Drawdown |
|-------|---------|------|----------------|---------------|
| 2012 | Nov 2012 | Dec 2013 | ~13 months | -86% |
| 2016 | Jul 2016 | Dec 2017 | ~17 months | -84% |
| 2020 | May 2020 | Nov 2021 | ~18 months | -77% |
| **2024** | **Apr 2024** | **Oct 2025** | **~18 months** | **-49% so far** |

The ATH at 18 months post-halving matches the 2020 cycle exactly. Two competing narratives:

1. **"Cycle is intact"** — Kaiko Research says the $126K → $65K correction aligns perfectly with historical patterns. The current drawdown is typical. [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/bitcoin-four-year-halving-cycle-analysis/)
2. **"Extended to 5 years"** — Macro factors (M2 cycles, corporate debt maturity, institutional ETF holders who hold through volatility) could stretch the cycle. A second peak may come Q2 2026. [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/macroeconomic-shifts-are-extending-bitcoins-cycle-to-2026-analysts-forecast/)

**Assessment:** The historical pattern suggests the cycle top is IN ($126K, Oct 2025). The extended-cycle thesis requires a macro catalyst (tariff resolution + rate cuts) to play out. Current price action looks more consistent with post-cycle bear market than mid-cycle correction.
