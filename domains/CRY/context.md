# CRY — Crypto & Digital Assets

## Scope
Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins, regulation, DeFi protocols, adoption milestones, stablecoin policy.

## Mental Models
- **Halving cycles** — Bitcoin's 4-year supply reduction cycle historically drives price
- **Liquidity flows** — crypto tracks global liquidity (M2 supply) with 12-18 month lag
- **Narrative cycles** — DeFi summer, NFT boom, AI tokens, memecoin mania — narratives drive allocation
- **Regulatory regime** — US regulatory stance drives institutional adoption
- **On-chain metrics** — MVRV, NUPL, exchange flows, miner behavior as leading indicators
- **ETF flows** — institutional demand proxy since BTC/ETH/SOL ETF approvals
- **Macro correlation** — BTC behaves as high-beta risk-on asset, amplifies Nasdaq moves (especially downside)

## Cycle Position (February 2026)

**We are likely past the cycle peak.**

- Last halving: April 20, 2024
- ATH: $126,198 on October 6, 2025 (~18 months post-halving — matches 2016, 2020 cycles exactly)
- Current: ~$64,800 (49% drawdown from ATH)
- Position: ~22 months post-halving, in post-peak correction phase
- Extended-cycle thesis exists (second peak Q2 2026) but is minority view — requires macro catalysts

## Price Models

**Stock-to-Flow (PlanB):** Lost credibility. BTC has deviated materially. Does not account for demand/macro. Historical reference only.

**Power Law (Santostasi):** Central trendline ~$122K for Feb 2026, floor ~$51K. Current price ($65K) in lower band — below trendline, above floor. Peak prediction of $210K by Jan 2026 missed badly. Good at levels, poor at timing.

**Analyst consensus:** $150K 2026 target from Standard Chartered, Bernstein, ARK (all lowered from earlier forecasts). Full range: $65K-$250K.
