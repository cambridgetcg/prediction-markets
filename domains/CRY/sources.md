# CRY — Sources

*Last updated: 2026-02-23*

## Price Data
- [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com) — Prices, market cap, historical data
- [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/price/) — Prices, news, analysis
- [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com) — Charts, technical analysis
- [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com) — Alternative to CoinMarketCap

## On-Chain Analytics

### Free
- [DeFiLlama](https://defillama.com) — TVL across all chains, protocol-level data, has API
- [CoinGlass](https://www.coinglass.com) — Exchange balances, MVRV, funding rates, open interest
- [L2BEAT](https://l2beat.com) — L2 health, adoption, risk assessment
- [Woobull Charts](http://charts.woobull.com) — BTC cycle indicators (MVRV, NVT, etc.)
- [Bitbo](https://charts.bitbo.io) — BTC on-chain charts (S2F, power law, etc.)
- [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com) — SOL network stats, staking
- [DexScreener](https://dexscreener.com) — DEX activity, meme coin tracking
- [Blockchain.com/charts](https://www.blockchain.com/charts) — Hash rate, block size, tx count

### Paid (worth considering)
- [Glassnode](https://glassnode.com) (~$40-800/mo) — Deep BTC/ETH on-chain (MVRV, SOPR, NUPL, exchange flows). Industry standard.
- [CryptoQuant](https://cryptoquant.com) (~$50-500/mo) — Exchange flows, miner data, derivatives metrics.
- [Nansen](https://www.nansen.ai) (~$100-2500/mo) — Smart money tracking, 500M+ labeled addresses.

## ETF Flows
- [SoSoValue](https://sosovalue.xyz/assets/etf/us-btc-spot) — Real-time ETF flow tracker (free)
- [CoinDesk ETF Tracker](https://www.coindesk.com) — Daily flow summaries
- [ETF.com](https://www.etf.com) — AUM, flows, fund details
- [Bloomberg Terminal](https://www.bloomberg.com) — Institutional-grade (paid)

## Sentiment
- [Alternative.me Fear & Greed Index](https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/) — Free, updated daily
- [CoinMarketCap Fear & Greed](https://coinmarketcap.com/charts/fear-and-greed-index/) — Alternative source

## Macro
- [FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org) — M2, interest rates, dollar index
- [TradingEconomics](https://tradingeconomics.com) — DXY, global macro indicators
- [CME FedWatch](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/interest-rates/cme-fedwatch-tool.html) — Rate cut probabilities
- [StreetStats](https://streetstats.finance/liquidity/money) — Global M2 money supply tracking

## Regulatory
- [SEC.gov](https://www.sec.gov) — Filings, enforcement actions, ETF decisions
- [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov) — Bill tracking (BITCOIN Act, GENIUS Act, CLARITY Act)
- [Latham & Watkins US Crypto Policy Tracker](https://www.lw.com/en/us-crypto-policy-tracker) — Comprehensive policy tracking
- [ESMA (MiCA)](https://www.esma.europa.eu) — EU crypto regulation

## News & Analysis
- [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com) — Industry standard crypto news
- [The Block](https://www.theblock.co) — Institutional-focused crypto news
- [Bloomberg Crypto](https://www.bloomberg.com/crypto) — Mainstream financial perspective
- [Messari](https://messari.io) — Research and data (free tier available)

## Key Data Points to Track (Weekly)

| Metric | Source | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| BTC ETF daily flows | SoSoValue | Institutional demand proxy |
| Fear & Greed Index | Alternative.me | Sentiment regime |
| DXY | TradingEconomics | Dollar/risk correlation |
| BTC exchange balances | CoinGlass | Supply squeeze indicator |
| MVRV ratio | CoinGlass / Glassnode | Valuation cycle position |
| Global M2 | FRED / StreetStats | Liquidity regime (12-18mo lag) |
| Miner hash rate | Blockchain.com | Network health / capitulation signal |
