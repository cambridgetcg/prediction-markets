# CRY — Recommended Strategies

*The emotional rollercoaster. Highest volatility, most emotional participants, most noise.*

## Why CRY Is High-Edge / High-Noise

- **Most emotional participants.** Crypto-native retail with high risk tolerance. Narrative-driven, FOMO/FUD cycles are the dominant pattern.
- **Highest volatility.** BTC swings 10-20%+ weekly. Low-liquidity markets can move 10-20% on a single $50K trade.
- **Wash trading is pervasive.** Columbia study found ~25% of Polymarket volume is fake, peaking at 60% weekly in Dec 2024. Volume signals are unreliable.
- **Oracle/resolution risk.** UMA whale governance attacks have forced incorrect resolutions. Settlement disputes are a real risk.
- **"By date" price target structure** creates time premium opportunities.

## Strategy Priority (ordered)

### 1. S002 Threshold Exit — **PRIMARY** (Score: 5/5)

CRY volatility means thresholds trigger constantly. Perfect terrain.

**Why it works here**: BTC price swings cause prediction market moves of 15-20%+ in days. Profit-taking thresholds (1.5x, 2x) are realistic and frequently hit. Without thresholds, you ride the rollercoaster back to zero.

**How to deploy**:
- Consider wider thresholds for CRY: S002c (2.0x profit / -50% stop) may be better than S002b due to noise amplitude
- Momentum override is critical: if 15%+ in your direction in 24h, tighten to breakeven (crypto can reverse that fast)
- Track: threshold hit rate, missed gains vs S001, whipsaw frequency

**Watch for**: False breakouts. Crypto is the domain where whipsaws are most common. S002d (trailing stop variant) may outperform here.

### 2. S004 Contrarian Overreaction — **PRIMARY** (Score: 5/5)

Crypto participants are the most emotional in any domain. Textbook overreaction terrain.

**Why it works here**: FUD cycles (exchange hack, regulation rumor, whale sell-off) cause panic spikes that consistently overshoot. FOMO cycles (ETF approval, halving narrative, institutional adoption) cause euphoric spikes that also overshoot. Mean reversion is the dominant pattern.

**How to deploy**:
- Trigger: 15%+ move in 24-48h driven by single event
- Classify: exchange hack/regulation news/whale move (emotional → fade) vs ETF approval/protocol upgrade (structural → don't fade)
- Enter 24-48h after spike, target 50% retracement
- 14-day time limit (crypto mean reversion is faster — consider shortening to 7-10 days)

**Watch for**: Cascading liquidations. In crypto, emotional spikes can become structural via forced selling (margin calls, liquidation cascades). If on-chain data shows massive liquidations, the move may be structural.

### 3. S006 Time Decay — **SECONDARY** (Score: 4/5)

"BTC above $X by [date]" markets are the canonical time decay opportunity.

**Why it works here**: Monthly BTC price targets ("BTC $200K by Dec 2026" at 6%) have clear time premium. Fear/greed overprices: during bull runs, YES is overpriced by euphoria; during bear runs, NO is underpriced by capitulation. The math is clean — calculate implied daily growth rate vs historical BTC daily returns.

**How to deploy**:
- Screen all "BTC above X by [date]" markets
- Calculate implied daily growth rate needed to reach target
- Compare to historical BTC daily returns for similar periods
- If implied growth rate > 2x historical → buy NO (overpriced by FOMO)
- If implied probability seems too low relative to BTC's position in halving cycle → buy YES

**Watch for**: Crypto tail events. BTC has genuine 50%+ drawdown potential. Negative skew is more extreme here than in GEO.

### 4. S005 Momentum — **SECONDARY** (Score: 4/5)

Crypto narratives persist for weeks. Bull/bear cycles have strong momentum effects.

**Why it works here**: When BTC breaks a key level, narrative momentum carries it further (self-fulfilling via FOMO). Halving cycle narratives persist for 12-18 months. The market digests information slowly when it conflicts with the prevailing narrative.

**How to deploy**:
- Trigger: BTC moves 10%+ over 7+ days on multiple events (not just one)
- Confirm: increasing volume, supporting narratives, correlated alt movements
- Enter on 3-5% pullback
- Trailing 15% stop (may need to widen to 20% for crypto volatility)
- Max 30-day hold

**Tension with S004**: The spike vs trend distinction is hardest in crypto. Decision rule: if the move happened in 1-2 days on one event → spike → S004. If 7+ days on multiple events → trend → S005. If ambiguous → skip.

## Strategies to Skip in CRY

- **S007 Arb (3/5)**: Cross-platform arb exists but wash trading inflates volume signals. Oracle risk adds resolution uncertainty that undermines arb math.
- **S001 Hold (3/5)**: Can out-calibrate retail, but noise is so extreme that the market may not converge to truth within your timeframe.
- **S008 Info Edge (3/5)**: On-chain data gives some edge, but the market is so noisy that correct information doesn't reliably move prices.
- **S003 Catalyst (3/5)**: Halving and ETF decisions are scheduled, but many crypto catalysts are surprise events (hacks, tweets, whale moves).

## The CRY Risk Rule

**Never let CRY positions exceed 20% of total portfolio.** The domain has the highest expected volatility and the highest risk of total loss (oracle manipulation, exchange failure, regulatory crackdown). Diversify across multiple CRY markets. Hard stops are not optional — they're survival.
