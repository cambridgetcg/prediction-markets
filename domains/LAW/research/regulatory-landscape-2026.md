## 2. Antitrust

### Google Search Monopoly
- **Liability**: Aug 2024 — Google illegally maintained search monopoly.
- **Remedies**: Sep 2025 — behavioral remedies only (ban exclusive distribution contracts, share search data). No Chrome/Android divestiture.
- **Appeal**: Google appealed liability (Jan 2026). DOJ + states cross-appealed remedies as insufficient (Feb 2026).
- **Next**: DC Circuit, arguments expected later 2026.

### Google Ad Tech
- **Liability**: Apr 2025 — Google willfully monopolized publisher ad server + ad exchange.
- **Remedies**: Ruling expected **Q1 2026 (imminent)**. Judge Brinkema skeptical of divestiture, leaning behavioral.

### Apple (DOJ v. Apple)
- Motion to dismiss denied June 2025. **Discovery phase**. No trial date set.

### Meta (FTC v. Meta)
- **Meta won** (Nov 2025). Judge ruled TikTok/YouTube are competitors, no monopoly.
- FTC appealing to DC Circuit (filed Jan 2026).

### Amazon (FTC v. Amazon)
- Trial pushed to **Feb 9, 2027**. Separate $2.5B dark patterns settlement.

### Trump DOJ/FTC Priorities
- "America First Antitrust" — pocketbook issues, content moderation scrutiny, labor markets
- **No new major Big Tech filings** — continuing inherited cases
- Merger enforcement softened: reinstated early termination grants

## 3. Crypto Regulation

### US Federal — Pro-Crypto Environment

**SEC Chair Paul Atkins** has fundamentally shifted the agency:
- Digital assets = top policy priority
- "Project Crypto" — joint SEC-CFTC initiative
- Self-custody = "foundational American value"
- Crypto in 401(k) accounts supported
- Token Taxonomy published (Jan 28, 2026)

**All three landmark SEC cases resolved:**
| Case | Outcome | Impact |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Coinbase** | Dismissed (Feb 2025) | No fines. Business model intact. |
| **Binance** | Dismissed with prejudice (May 2025) | Cannot be refiled. Separate $4.3B DOJ settlement stands. |
| **Ripple/XRP** | $50M settlement (Aug 2025) | XRP NOT a security in retail. Spot XRP ETFs now live ($1.4B+ inflows). |

**GENIUS Act (Stablecoins)** — SIGNED INTO LAW (Jul 18, 2025):
- First comprehensive federal stablecoin law
- 1:1 backing required. Payment stablecoins are NOT securities or commodities.
- **July 18, 2026**: Implementing regulations deadline
- **January 2027**: Full enforcement

**Strategic Bitcoin Reserve** — EO signed March 2025:
- ~328,372 BTC held (largest known state holder globally)
- BITCOIN Act (1M BTC over 5 years) pending in Senate — no floor vote yet

**Market Structure Legislation**:
- House passed CLARITY Act (Jul 2025)
- Senate Banking + Agriculture committees both advancing bills (Jan 2026)
- Must reconcile before midterms (Nov 2026)

### State-Level Crypto
- **New Hampshire**: First state crypto reserve law. First Bitcoin-backed municipal bond ($100M).
- **Texas**: First state to actually purchase BTC (via BlackRock IBIT ETF).
- **California**: Digital Financial Assets Law takes effect **July 1, 2026**.

## 5. Prediction Market Legality

### Federal: Most Favorable Environment Ever

**CFTC Chairman Selig** — openly pro-prediction markets:
1. Withdrew proposed ban on political/sports event contracts
2. Declared prediction markets are NOT gambling; CFTC has exclusive jurisdiction
3. New rulemaking announced (Jan 29, 2026) — four-part regulatory agenda
4. Developing insider trading rules
5. SDNY expects fraud prosecutions related to prediction market trading

### Polymarket
- **Returned to US** (Dec 2025, invite-only) via QCEX acquisition ($112M for CFTC licenses)
- State challenges: Nevada Gaming Control Board civil complaint (Jan 2026)
- ~$12B traded across Kalshi + Polymarket in Dec 2025 alone (400% YoY)

### Kalshi
- **19 active federal lawsuits** (6 offensive, 8 defensive, 5 class actions)
- Won in NJ + TN (sports contracts = swaps under CFTC)
- Lost in MA + MD (states prevailed)
- Core question: derivatives (federal) or gambling (state)?

### Could Regulation Kill Polymarket?
**Very unlikely under current admin.** Risks:
1. State enforcement patchwork (NV, MA, TN cease-and-desists)
2. Insider trading scandal triggering backlash
3. Administration change reversing CFTC stance
4. Circuit split resolution (SCOTUS possibly ~2028)

### International Status
- **Europe broadly hostile**: France, Belgium, Italy, Poland, Portugal, Hungary, Netherlands, Ukraine — all banned or restricted
- **UK**: Requires gambling license; no prediction market has obtained one
- **Robinhood**: In talks with UK FCA and EU regulators about prediction market expansion

## 8. Assessment: Prediction Market Regulatory Risk

**Current risk level: LOW (under this administration)**

The three-layer protection:
1. **CFTC Chairman** actively championing prediction markets
2. **Trump admin** deregulatory stance
3. **Polymarket** now properly licensed via QCEX

**Risks to monitor:**
1. **State-level enforcement** (ongoing — TN, NV, MA active)
2. **Insider trading scandal** (SDNY signaling prosecutions)
3. **2026 midterms** (could shift Congressional composition)
4. **2028 presidential election** (new admin could reverse everything)
5. **Circuit split escalation** (could reach SCOTUS ~2028)

**Net assessment**: The regulatory window for prediction markets is **wide open but time-limited**. This administration is the most favorable possible; future administrations may not be. The industry is racing to establish legitimacy and mainstream adoption before political winds potentially shift.
