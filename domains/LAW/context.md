# LAW — Legal & Regulatory

## Scope
Court rulings, investigations, regulatory actions, constitutional questions, antitrust, crypto regulation, AI regulation, prediction market legality.

## Mental Models
- **Precedent** — courts follow precedent; radical departures are rare (but *Humphrey's Executor* may be overturned this term)
- **Regulatory capture** — regulators favor incumbents, but current admin has broken this pattern (CFTC championing prediction markets, SEC championing crypto)
- **Political courts** — SCOTUS 6-3 conservative, but tariff ruling showed 6-3 can split unexpectedly
- **Enforcement lag** — investigation → charge → trial → appeal takes years. Antitrust cases span 3-5 years.
- **Regulatory arbitrage** — money flows to friendliest jurisdiction. US is currently friendliest for crypto/prediction markets.
- **Federal-state tension** — defining dynamic of 2026. Federal preemption vs state enforcement on AI, crypto, prediction markets.
- **Administration window** — current regulatory environment is time-limited. Policies reverse with administration changes.
