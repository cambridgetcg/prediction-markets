# ECO — Sources

## Central Banks
- **Federal Reserve** — [FOMC statements](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm), dot plots, minutes, press conferences
- **ECB** — rate decisions, press conferences
- **BoE** — MPC minutes, Monetary Policy Report

## Data — Primary
- **FRED** (St. Louis Fed) — all US economic data ([fred.stlouisfed.org](https://fred.stlouisfed.org))
- **BLS** — [employment/CPI](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/current_year.asp) (release schedule)
- **BEA** — [GDP, PCE](https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule) (release schedule)
- **CME FedWatch** — [rate probabilities from futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/interest-rates/cme-fedwatch-tool.html)
- **Cleveland Fed** — [inflation nowcasting](https://www.clevelandfed.org/indicators-and-data/inflation-nowcasting)

## Data — Labor
- **BLS JOLTS** — [job openings, quits, hires](https://www.bls.gov/jlt/news.htm)
- **BLS Employment Situation** — [NFP, unemployment](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)

## Analysis
- **Bloomberg** — terminal-grade economic analysis
- **Goldman Sachs Insights** — [rate cut outlook](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/the-outlook-for-fed-rate-cuts-in-2026)
- **J.P. Morgan Insights** — [FOMC previews](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/markets-and-economy/economy/fed-meeting-january-2026)
- **Tax Foundation** — [tariff tracker](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/trump-tariffs-trade-war/)
- **Yale Budget Lab** — [state of US tariffs](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/state-us-tariffs-february-20-2026)
- **CFR** — [Fed leadership analysis](https://www.cfr.org/articles/why-kevin-warsh-wont-revolutionize-the-federal-reserve)
- **The Economist** — macro overview
- **Adam Tooze Chartbook** — excellent macro newsletter
