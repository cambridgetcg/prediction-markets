# ECO — Economics & Monetary Policy

## Scope
Central bank decisions, interest rates, inflation, GDP, employment, trade policy, recession risk, fiscal policy.

## Mental Models
- **Taylor Rule** — rates should track inflation gap + output gap
- **Expectations anchoring** — what people expect matters as much as what is
- **Forward guidance** — central banks telegraph moves; surprises are rare
- **Yield curve** — spread between short/long rates signals recession risk
- **Phillips curve (modified)** — unemployment-inflation tradeoff, but not stable
- **Fiscal dominance** — when govt debt is so large it constrains monetary policy
- **FedWatch probabilities** — CME tool tracks rate expectations from futures

## Key Context (2026)
*Updated: 2026-02-23*

- **Fed funds rate**: 3.50%–3.75% (held since Jan 28, 2026; after three 25bp cuts Sep-Dec 2025)
- **Inflation**: CPI core 2.5% YoY (Jan 2026, slowest since Mar 2021), but PCE core 3.0% (Dec 2025, reaccelerated). CPI and PCE are diverging — Fed targets PCE
- **Shelter**: lagging indicator, forecast to slow to 2.1% annualized by H2 2026 — key disinflationary tailwind, but slow
- **Labor market**: NFP +130K (Jan), unemployment 4.3%, JOLTS openings at 6.5M (lowest since Sep 2020). Cooling but not crashing
- **Tariffs**: SCOTUS struck down IEEPA tariffs Feb 20; Trump imposing 15% global tariff in response. Effective rate ~16.9% (highest since 1932). Section 232 (25% on cars) remains. Adds ~0.3pp to inflation in 2026
- **Fed leadership**: Kevin Warsh nominated Jan 30, confirmation blocked by Tillis over DOJ probe into Powell. Transition expected May 2026
- **QT**: Ended Dec 1, 2025. Fed now reinvesting all principal payments. Balance sheet discussions at March FOMC
- **Dot plot** (Dec 2025): Median implies one more 25bp cut in 2026 (to 3.25-3.50%)
- **Forward curve**: Market prices 1-2 cuts in 2026, likely starting June; 43% chance of 3+ cuts
- **US fiscal position**: high deficits, debt ceiling debates
- **Global**: ECB, BoE cutting faster than Fed; BoJ on tightening path
