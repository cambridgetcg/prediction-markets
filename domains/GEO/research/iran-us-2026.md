## 3. Iran Nuclear Timeline

### Pre-Strike Status (May 2025)

- **408.6 kg** of uranium enriched to 60% (90% of the work toward weapons-grade)
- Total enriched uranium: **9,247.6 kg**
- Producing 60% HEU at **~1.1 kg/day**
- Breakout time: **~1 week** for first weapon; ~2 weeks for 8 weapons

### Post-Strike Status (June 2025 — Present)

**Operation Rising Lion** (Israel, June 13) + **Operation Midnight Hammer** (US, June 22) targeted Fordow, Natanz, and Isfahan:
- B-2s dropped 14 GBU-57 MOPs on Fordow. USS Georgia launched 30+ Tomahawks at Isfahan.
- Above-ground structures severely damaged
- **BUT** the ~400 kg of 60% HEU **survived** the strikes
- Centrifuges **largely intact** underground
- DIA initially assessed "maybe a few months" setback; later revised to **1-2 years**

### Current Status (February 2026)

- Iran is **not actively enriching** (IAEA satellite monitoring confirms)
- However, **"movement detected near stockpile"** — ~400 kg of 60% HEU remains
- IAEA has been **expelled** since June 25, 2025 (Iranian parliament suspended all safeguards)
- Only access: Bushehr civilian reactor. **No access** to Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan, or stockpile.
- Iran **refused** to export enriched uranium to Russia or elsewhere

### Weaponization Signals

- **December 2025**: Italian think tank ISPI reported Khamenei authorized miniaturized warhead development in October 2025. Single source, unverified by US intelligence.
- **February 2026**: Satellite imagery shows Iran building **concrete sarcophagus** over Parchin (weapons-related explosive testing site) and covering it with soil.
- ISIS (think tank) assesses probability Iran will build nuclear weapons at **~50%**.
- Bulletin of Atomic Scientists: Iran could potentially build weapons **without further enrichment** using existing 60% stockpile in implosion design (technically harder but feasible).

### Delivery Systems

- Missile stockpile rebuilt to **~2,000 missiles** (pre-war levels)
- Factories operating "around the clock"
- Key systems: Shahab-3 (1,950 km), Kheibar Shekan (1,450 km, solid fuel), Khorramshahr (2,000 km)
- February 5: Iran deployed Khorramshahr-4 in underground facility — shift from defensive to offensive doctrine
- No ICBM capability; could develop by ~2035

## 8. Time Decay Analysis

### Implied Daily Probability of Strike

Using P(no strike by day N) = (1-p)^N:

| Window | P(strike) | Days | Implied Daily p |
|--------|-----------|------|-----------------|
| By Feb 28 | 18% | 5 | **3.89%** |
| By Mar 31 | 57% | 36 | **2.31%** |
| By Jun 30 | 66% | 127 | **0.85%** |

### Consistency Check

The daily rate **drops sharply** over time → market believes probability is **heavily front-loaded**.

**Cross-check**: If the Mar 31 daily rate (2.31%) held for Feb 23-28 (5 days):
- P(by Feb 28) = 1 - 0.9769^5 = 11.0%
- But market says 18% → the **next 5 days are priced as significantly more dangerous** than the average day through March.

**Conditional check**: P(strike Mar 1-31 | no strike by Feb 28):
- = (0.57 - 0.18) / (1 - 0.18) = **47.6%**
- Daily rate for March: 1 - 0.524^(1/31) = **2.05%/day**

**Conditional check**: P(strike Apr 1 - Jun 30 | no strike by Mar 31):
- = (0.66 - 0.57) / (1 - 0.57) = **20.9%**
- Daily rate for Apr-Jun: 1 - 0.791^(1/91) = **0.26%/day**

### Interpretation

The market structure implies:
1. **Next 5 days (Feb 23-28)**: ~3.9%/day — Trump's deadline window, Feb 26 talks critical
2. **March (if no strike by Feb 28)**: ~2.05%/day — deadline expiration, military assets fully positioned
3. **Apr-Jun (if no strike by Mar 31)**: ~0.26%/day — if it hasn't happened by April, probability drops dramatically

This is consistent with the view that **the current crisis has a defined window**. If talks produce an interim framework by early March, the acute phase passes. If they don't, strikes become likely in the March timeframe when all military assets are in position and the deadline has expired.

## Bull Case (Why Strikes Are Likely)

1. **Precedent already set**: US struck Iran in June 2025. The psychological, legal, and political barriers have been broken. This is "round 2," not "first strike."
2. **Force posture is strike-ready**: 2 carriers, 120+ aircraft, B-2s at Diego Garcia, 300+ Tomahawks on submarines. Largest buildup since 2003.
3. **Ultimatum issued**: Trump gave 10-15 days (from Feb 19). His deadlines in the first Trump-Iran cycle (April 2025) led to war when they expired.
4. **Nuclear stockpile survived**: ~400 kg of 60% HEU still exists, sufficient for ~10 weapons. IAEA has no access. Iran building bunkers. The original rationale for strikes hasn't been resolved.
5. **Administration track record**: Venezuela (Jan 2026), Southern Spear (ongoing), June 2025 Iran strikes. This team acts on threats.
6. **Israel is a willing partner**: Cabinet authorized strikes Jan 5. Netanyahu pushing for "round 2." IAF preparing.
7. **Diplomatic gap is enormous**: "Zero enrichment forever" vs. "right to enrich" — this cannot close in 10-15 days.
8. **Domestic politics**: Trump benefits from appearing strong. No significant anti-war constituency in the current political environment.

## Bear Case (Why Strikes May Not Happen)

1. **Talks are still active**: 3rd round scheduled Feb 26 in Geneva. Positive signals from both sides ("guiding principles agreed," "encouraging signals," "clearer path").
2. **Oil price risk**: Strikes would push oil above $80-100, undermining Trump's cheap-gas priority. Strait of Hormuz disruption is a genuine economic risk.
