## Primary Strategy: S006 + S008 Combination

### S006 on "By Date" Event Markets
- Volcano, hurricane landfall, natural disaster markets all have time-decay structures
- Base rates are well-established and markets consistently overprice dramatic events (availability bias)
- Best edge: VEI≥6 volcano NO (6-7% edge over base rate)

### S008 on Temperature / Ice Markets
- Understanding ENSO phase, Met Office forecasts, and climate models provides genuine info edge
- Monthly temperature series offers regular resolution cycles for calibration
- 2026 hottest year ranking market ($1.4M volume) is the deepest ENV market
