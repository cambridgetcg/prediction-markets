# ENV — Environment & Energy

## Scope
Climate targets, energy policy, natural disasters, resource markets, renewable deployment, carbon pricing, food security, farmland.

## Mental Models
- **Energy transition S-curves** — Solar/wind/batteries on exponential deployment curves. China driving >50% of global additions.
- **Tipping points** — Non-linear climate thresholds. Coral reefs likely already tipping. Greenland/WAIS may be underway. AMOC contested but median tipping ~2055.
- **Jevons paradox** — Efficiency gains can increase consumption (relevant to AI datacenter demand).
- **Stranded assets** — Fossil fuel assets that can't be monetized under climate policy. But US policy in full retreat at federal level.
- **Energy security vs climate** — Geopolitical shocks reset priorities (EU banning Russian gas, tariff impacts on solar).
- **Insurance death spiral** — Extreme weather → insurer withdrawals → uninsurable risk → market disruption. Already playing out in CA/FL.
- **ENSO forecasting** — El Nino/La Nina phase is strongest predictor of annual temperature anomaly. Crucial for temperature markets.
