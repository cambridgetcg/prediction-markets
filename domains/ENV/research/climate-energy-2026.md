## 1. Climate Tracking

### Temperature Records

| Year | Anomaly vs Pre-Industrial | Rank |
|------|--------------------------|------|
| 2024 | +1.55C | #1 (hottest ever) |
| 2023 | +1.45C | #2 |
| 2025 | +1.44C | #3 |

2024 was the first year to breach 1.5C as an annual average. 2025 came in just below at 1.44C — remarkably warm given La Nina conditions, which typically cool the planet. The 3-year average (2023-2025) is 1.48C — first time a 3-year period exceeds 1.5C.

The Paris Agreement defines the threshold as a long-term average (~20 years), not a single year. By that measure, it hasn't been formally breached. But Copernicus says sustained breach is "basically inevitable."

Sources: [WMO](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2025-was-one-of-warmest-years-record), [Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-2025-was-third-hottest-year-record), [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/temperatures-rising-nasa-confirms-2024-warmest-year-on-record/)

### 2026 Early Trends

- **January 2026**: 5th-warmest January on record. +1.12C above 20th-century average.

- **ENSO forecast**: 96% chance of ENSO-neutral Feb-Apr 2026. El Nino favored by mid-2026 (58%). El Nino developing would push temps higher in H2 2026.
- **Met Office prediction**: 2026 will be ~1.46C above pre-industrial, likely 2nd warmest on record.

Sources: [NOAA](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/news/global-climate-202601), [CPC ENSO](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml), [Severe Weather EU](https://www.severe-weather.eu/long-range-2/la-nina-dissolve-el-nino-forecast-weather-shift-2026-united-states-canada-europe-fa/)

| System | Status |
|--------|--------|

Key constraint on full IRA repeal: 80% of $289B in clean tech investment went to Republican districts.

### EU — Targets Survive, Implementation Weakening

- **55% emissions reduction by 2030** target legally binding. But Omnibus I (Feb 2025) rolled back CSRD, Taxonomy, CSDDD safeguards.
- **CBAM**: Entered definitive compliance phase Jan 1, 2026. Certificate pricing based on EU ETS. De minimis exemption for <50 tonnes/year.
- **EU ETS**: ~EUR 81/t. Heading toward EUR 100+ by 2027. Supply 8% lower in 2026 (mechanical tightening).
- **Russian gas ban**: Legally binding stepwise prohibition signed Jan 26, 2026. LNG ban by end 2026, pipeline ban by autumn 2027.

### China — Emissions Likely Peaked

- CO2 emissions flat or falling for 21 months as of late 2025. Full-year 2025: -0.3%.
- Added 434 GW of solar+wind in 2025 alone. Combined wind+solar capacity surpassed coal for first time.
- Coal still 60%+ of electricity, but power-sector coal use declined 3.4% in H1 2025.
- Oil consumption expected to peak in 2026. Coal plateau from 2027.
- **This is historic** — world's largest emitter plateauing years ahead of official "before 2030" target.

Sources: [Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-co2-emissions-have-now-been-flat-or-falling-for-21-months/), [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/01/26/russian-gas-imports-council-gives-final-greenlight-to-a-stepwise-ban/), [TIME](https://time.com/7262600/how-trump-is-trying-to-undo-the-inflation-reduction-act/)

## 10. Top 3 Environmental/Energy Predictions for 2026

### 1. 2026 will be the 2nd warmest year on record (~1.46C anomaly)
**Confidence: 70%**

La Nina is dissolving. Neutral conditions through spring, El Nino developing by mid-year. Met Office forecasts 1.46C. This would rank 2nd behind 2024's 1.55C and ahead of 2025's 1.44C. The main uncertainty is whether El Nino develops strongly enough to push 2026 above 2024 (unlikely but possible) or whether cooler conditions persist longer than expected.

### 2. Global solar installations will exceed 600 GW in 2026
**Confidence: 80%**

2025 saw ~700 GW (BloombergNEF). China alone is deploying at staggering pace. Even with US tariff headwinds slowing domestic growth, global deployment is on an exponential curve. IEA's "plateau at 540 GW" forecast has been wrong before — the S-curve is still in its steep phase. Battery storage at $65/MWh LCOS removes the intermittency objection.

### 3. No VEI ≥6 volcanic eruption in 2026
**Confidence: 99%**

Base rate of ~1 per century. No current indicators of an imminent large eruption. This is the most confident prediction and the most tradeable (Polymarket has it at 8% YES).
