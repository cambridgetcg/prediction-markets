# ENV — Sources

*Last updated: 2026-02-23*

## Climate Data

### Temperature
- [NASA GISTEMP](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/) — Global temperature anomaly (monthly, resolves Polymarket temp markets)
- [Copernicus Climate Change Service](https://climate.copernicus.eu/) — ERA5 reanalysis, European standard
- [NOAA Global Climate Reports](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/monitoring/monthly-report/global/) — Monthly global reports
- [WMO](https://wmo.int/) — Annual climate statements, official records
- [Berkeley Earth](https://berkeleyearth.org/) — Independent temperature analysis

### ENSO (Critical for Temperature Forecasting)
- [CPC ENSO Diagnostic Discussion](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml) — Monthly ENSO forecasts
- [NOAA ENSO Forecasts](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/) — Probability outlooks

### Sea Ice
- [NSIDC (National Snow and Ice Data Center)](https://nsidc.org/) — Arctic sea ice extent (resolves Polymarket ice markets)
- [NSIDC Charctic](https://nsidc.org/arcticseaicenews/charctic-interactive-sea-ice-graph/) — Interactive sea ice comparison tool

### Extreme Weather
- [Munich Re NatCatSERVICE](https://www.munichre.com/en/solutions/for-industry-clients/natcatservice.html) — Insured/economic loss data
- [Aon Catastrophe Insight](https://www.aon.com/en/insights/reports/climate-and-catastrophe-report) — Annual loss reports
- [NOAA Billion-Dollar Disasters](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/billions/) — US disaster database
- [EM-DAT](https://www.emdat.be/) — International disaster database

### Tipping Points
- [Earth Commission](https://earthcommission.org/) — Safe and just Earth system boundaries
- [RealClimate](https://www.realclimate.org/) — Climate science blog (AMOC, tipping points)

## Energy Transition

### Renewables
- [IRENA](https://www.irena.org/) — Renewable capacity statistics, LCOE data (annual)
- [Ember](https://ember-energy.org/) — Real-time electricity data, renewable tracking (free, excellent)
- [BloombergNEF](https://about.bnef.com/) — Battery prices, solar forecast (some paywalled)
- [IEA](https://www.iea.org/) — World Energy Outlook, Global EV Outlook
- [Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA)](https://seia.org/) — US solar data
- [Global Wind Energy Council (GWEC)](https://gwec.net/) — Wind capacity reports
- [PV Magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/) — Solar industry news

### Nuclear
- [World Nuclear Association](https://world-nuclear.org/) — Reactor database, construction status
- [NRC](https://www.nrc.gov/) — US reactor licensing, SMR approvals
- [IAEA PRIS](https://pris.iaea.org/) — Power reactor information system

### Battery Storage
- [BloombergNEF Battery Survey](https://about.bnef.com/) — Annual pack price survey
- [Energy Storage News](https://www.energy-storage.news/) — Storage industry tracking
- [SEIA/Wood Mackenzie US Solar Market Insight](https://seia.org/) — US storage deployment

### EVs
- [IEA Global EV Outlook](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2025) — Annual (released ~June)
- [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/) — EV sales tracking
- [EV-Volumes](https://www.ev-volumes.com/) — Global EV sales data
- [The EV Report](https://theevreport.com/) — Country-level data

### AI / Datacenter Energy
- [IEA Energy and AI](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/) — Demand projections
- [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/) — Datacenter electricity forecasts
- [EIA Today in Energy](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/) — US datacenter and grid data

## Policy

### US
- [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/) — Legislation tracking
- [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/) — Executive orders, regulations
- [EPA.gov](https://www.epa.gov/) — Regulatory actions
- [C2ES](https://www.c2es.org/) — Climate policy analysis
- [E&E News](https://www.eenews.net/) — Energy/environment policy news

### EU
- [European Commission Climate Action](https://climate.ec.europa.eu/) — Green Deal, ETS, CBAM
- [ICAP (International Carbon Action Partnership)](https://icapcarbonaction.com/) — Carbon market tracker
- [Trading Economics EU Carbon](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/carbon) — ETS price data
- [EU Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/) — Legislative decisions

### Global
- [Climate Action Tracker](https://climateactiontracker.org/) — Country-level emissions tracking
- [Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/) — Climate science and policy analysis (excellent)
- [World Bank Carbon Pricing](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/state-and-trends-of-carbon-pricing) — Global carbon pricing status

## Food Security & Farmland

### Food Prices
- [World Bank Commodity Markets](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) — Price data
- [FAO Food Price Index](https://www.fao.org/worldfoodsituation/foodpricesindex/) — Monthly
- [USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/) — US food price outlook

### UK Farmland
- [Savills Farmland Value Survey](https://www.savills.co.uk/landing-pages/farmland-market.aspx) — Quarterly UK farmland prices
- [Strutt & Parker Farmland Market Review](https://rural.struttandparker.com/) — Semi-annual
- [Carter Jonas Rural Market](https://www.carterjonas.co.uk/rural/research) — Quarterly
- [Farmers Weekly Land Markets](https://www.fwi.co.uk/business/markets-and-trends/land-markets) — News and data
- [East Anglian Daily Times](https://www.eadt.co.uk/) — Suffolk-specific farmland coverage
