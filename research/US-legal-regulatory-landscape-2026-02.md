## 3. Prediction Market Legality

### Federal Landscape

#### CFTC Stance (Pro-Prediction Markets Under Trump)

The CFTC under Chairman Michael Selig has done a 180 from the Biden era:

1. **Withdrew prohibitory rule**: The CFTC withdrew its proposed rule that would have banned political and sports event contracts
2. **"Exclusive jurisdiction" claim**: Selig declared prediction markets are NOT gambling and that the CFTC has exclusive federal jurisdiction
3. **New rulemaking announced (Jan 29, 2026)**: Four-part regulatory agenda to support "responsible development" of event contract markets
4. **Insider trading focus**: CFTC developing rules around insider trading in prediction markets; in contact with platforms and sports leagues
5. **Criminal enforcement**: US Attorney for SDNY stated (Feb 5, 2026) he expects fraud prosecutions related to prediction market trading

### Could Regulation Kill Polymarket?

**Short answer: Very unlikely under current federal administration, but state-level risk persists.**

**Why it's unlikely to be killed:**
- CFTC Chairman Selig is openly pro-prediction markets
- Trump admin deregulatory stance favors innovation
- Polymarket now has proper CFTC licensing via QCEX acquisition
- $12B monthly volume creates political constituency

**Remaining risks:**
1. **State enforcement patchwork**: States like NV, MA, and others treating it as unlicensed gambling
2. **Insider trading scandals**: SDNY eyeing fraud prosecutions; any major scandal could trigger backlash
3. **Proposed legislation**: Rep. Ritchie Torres introduced "Public Integrity in Financial Prediction Markets Act of 2026" banning government officials from trading on outcomes they influence
4. **Administration change**: A future Democratic admin could reverse CFTC stance
5. **Circuit split resolution**: If SCOTUS eventually rules against federal preemption, states could regulate freely

## 4. Trump Admin Regulatory Approach

- **EO 14219 "DOGE Deregulatory Initiative" (Feb 19, 2025)**: Agencies must work with DOGE to review all regulations and prepare to rescind/modify those inconsistent with law and administration policy.
- **EO "AI National Policy Framework" (Dec 11, 2025)**: Preempts state AI regulation; directs FCC/FTC to create federal AI standards within 90 days.
- **Total**: 243 executive orders, 57 memoranda, 128 proclamations signed as of Feb 19, 2026.

## Key Takeaways for Prediction Markets

1. **The federal regulatory environment is the most favorable ever** for prediction markets. CFTC Chairman Selig is actively championing the industry, withdrawing restrictive rules, and asserting exclusive jurisdiction against states.

2. **The biggest risk is the state-level patchwork.** 19 active lawsuits against Kalshi alone, with conflicting court rulings creating a circuit split. This will likely take years to resolve (SCOTUS possibly by 2028).

3. **Polymarket is now CFTC-licensed and operating in the US** (invite-only since Dec 2025). The QCEX acquisition was the key unlock. State-level enforcement (especially NV, MA) remains a concern.

4. **Insider trading is the next regulatory frontier.** CFTC and SDNY are both signaling enforcement. A major scandal could shift political winds.

5. **Europe is hostile.** France, Belgium, Italy, Poland, Portugal, Hungary, Netherlands, and Ukraine have all banned or restricted prediction markets. The UK treats them as gambling. MiCA complicates crypto-based platforms.

6. **The Trump v. Slaughter ruling (expected June 2026)** could further empower the executive to control agency direction, making the CFTC's pro-market stance even more durable for the duration of this administration.

7. **Administration change is the tail risk.** A Democratic CFTC chairman could reverse course. But the industry's rapid growth ($12B/month) and mainstreaming (Robinhood entering the space) create increasing political resistance to a crackdown.
