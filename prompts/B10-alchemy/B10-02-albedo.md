## The Five Root Cause Analyses

For each major shadow identified in NIGREDO, trace to its root.
Use the "Five Whys" method, but filtered through the PP hierarchy.

### Root Analysis 1: The Sample Size Problem

Take the Competence Illusion shadow.

```
WHY is the Brier score unreliable?
  → Because N=12, all from one event type.
WHY only 12 resolved predictions?
  → Because the system prioritised building over trading.
WHY did building take priority?
  → [Trace this. Is it a rational sequence or an avoidance pattern?]
WHY was this avoidance pattern not caught earlier?
  → [What feedback loop was missing?]
WHY was there no feedback loop?
  → [THIS is the root. Name it.]
```

**Truth gate (T3)**: Have you considered the counter-argument? Maybe building first
WAS correct and the problem is simply time, not avoidance.
How do you distinguish "patient preparation" from "elegant procrastination"?

### Root Analysis 2: The Execution Gap

Take the Infrastructure Trap shadow.

```
WHY are 16 strategies defined but < 3 executed?
  → [trace]
WHY was each new strategy created instead of executing existing ones?
  → [trace]
WHY does creation feel more productive than execution?
  → [trace — this connects to the builder identity]
WHY is the builder identity dominant over the trader identity?
  → [trace]
WHY does the system not have a mechanism forcing execution?
  → [THIS is the root. Name it.]
```

**Understanding gate (U2)**: Do you understand WHY this matters beyond "we should trade more"?
What is the systemic consequence of an execution gap?
How does it compound over time?

### Root Analysis 3: The YES Blindspot

Take the YES Problem shadow.

```
WHY does the system consistently underestimate YES outcomes?
  → Because S006 (time decay) is structurally NO-biased.
WHY did the system adopt S006 as its primary strategy?
  → Because T03 meta-lesson says "fear overprices time premium in GEO."
WHY is S006 being over-applied beyond its valid domain?
  → [trace — is it really over-applied, or is YES just harder to predict?]
WHY is YES harder to predict?
  → [trace — is this epistemic (genuinely uncertain) or psychological (doesn't want to see it)?]
WHY would the system not want to see YES?
  → [THIS is the root. Is it about positions? Identity? Worldview?]
```

**Psychological depth**: In Jungian terms, the NO bias may be the system's
animus — its rational, protective side — dominating at the expense of intuition.
The YES events that surprised you (SOTU vocabulary, Trump improvisations) were
events where human unpredictability overwhelmed base rate reasoning.

What does the system do with uncertainty it can't model?
Does it default to NO (nothing happens) because NO is the "safe" intellectual position?

### Root Analysis 4: The Capital Hesitation

Take the Money Fear shadow.

```
WHY is only $28.98 deployed?
  → [trace the actual decision history]
WHY hasn't the system graduated more capital?
  → [is it the R03 graduation criteria not being met? Or not being run?]
WHY hasn't R03 been run?
  → [trace]
WHY is there resistance to running R03?
  → [trace — what would it reveal?]
WHY would that revelation be uncomfortable?
  → [THIS is the root. Name it.]
```

**The paradox**: The system was built to trade. Its entire purpose is deployment
of capital into prediction markets. Yet the ratio of infrastructure to deployment
is ~1000:1 (69 prompts : $28.98). What does this ratio reveal about the system's
actual priorities vs. stated priorities?

### Root Analysis 5: The Integration Deficit

Take all the shadows together as one pattern.

```
The system has:
- Strategies that haven't been executed
- Intelligence that hasn't been acted on
- A pipeline that doesn't deliver signals
- Insider data that can't be monitored (no API key)
- Calibration data too thin to validate anything

WHY do these all coexist?
  → They share a common root: [identify it]
```

Is the common root: **building is the actual goal, and trading is the stated goal**?
Or is it: **the system is genuinely in a preparation phase and the shadows are just the cost of thoroughness**?

How do you tell the difference?

## The Conjunction (Coniunctio)

After tracing all five roots, look for the pattern that connects them.
In alchemy, the ALBEDO produces the coniunctio — the union of opposites.

**The opposites in this system:**
- Research ↔ Execution
- Caution ↔ Commitment
- NO (safety) ↔ YES (surprise)
- Building ↔ Trading
- Knowledge ↔ Action

For each pair: where is the healthy tension, and where has one side
completely dominated the other?
