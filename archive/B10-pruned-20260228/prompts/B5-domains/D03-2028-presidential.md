Research the 2028 US presidential election landscape. This is one of the highest-liquidity markets on Polymarket:

## Research Tasks

1. **Republican field**:
   - J.D. Vance (41%): VP incumbency advantage. What are his strengths/weaknesses as a candidate? Approval ratings? Policy positions? Does he have Trump's base?

2. **Democratic field**:
   - Why is the field so open? (No clear frontrunner above 2%)

   - Democratic Party dynamics: progressive vs moderate lane, identity politics, donor networks
   - Historical: How do out-of-power parties select nominees? Patterns from 2008, 2016, 2020

3. **Structural factors**:
   - Incumbency effect (Vance would be quasi-incumbent)
   - Economic conditions forecast for 2028
   - Demographic shifts (Gen Z fully voting age)
   - Electoral college map evolution
   - Primary calendar and early state dynamics

4. **Historical base rates for early polling**:
   - How predictive are prediction markets 2.5 years out?
   - Who was leading at this point in previous cycles? How often did they win?
   - What typically changes between now and the election?

5. **Value opportunities**:
   - Where is the market most likely wrong?
   - Which candidates are underpriced relative to their realistic chances?
   - What events in 2026-2027 would most shift the probabilities?
