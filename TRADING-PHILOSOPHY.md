## Core Principle

**The closer an asset is to a known resolution mechanism with a hard date, the more comfortable I am.**

Prediction markets are structurally superior for systematic trading because:
1. Binary outcomes — no "how much" uncertainty, only "whether"
2. Hard expiry — capital efficiency is measurable
3. Information edge is well-defined — who knows more about X than the crowd?
4. No counterparty risk beyond platform (vs. equities: management, macro, sentiment)

## Preferred Holding Durations by Asset Class

### Prediction Markets ✅ Primary focus
| Duration | Comfort | Use Case |
|----------|---------|----------|
| <1 week | High | Near-certain resolution (calibration trades, time-decay harvest) |
| 1–6 weeks | **Highest** | Hard-date events: elections, summits, court verdicts, deadlines |
| 6 weeks–3 months | Medium | Structural thesis plays (diplomatic tracks, policy cycles) |
| 3–12 months | Low | Only with very high conviction + strong resolution rules edge |
| >12 months | Avoid | Capital lock-up, thesis decay, unknowable black swans |

**Edge sources ranked:**
1. Resolution rules misread by market (e.g. Maduro "de jure" trap)
2. Known upcoming catalyst mispriced (Geneva talks, Fed meetings)
3. Time-decay on near-certain outcomes
4. Contrarian fade of overreaction (S004 — 58% negative autocorrelation)

### Equities ⚠️ Selective
| Duration | Comfort | Use Case |
|----------|---------|----------|
| <2 weeks | Low | Noise dominates; HFT/MM advantage |
| 2 weeks–3 months | Medium | Event-driven: earnings, FDA, product launch |
| 3–12 months | High | Trend + catalyst convergence |
| 1–3 years | High | Structural thematics (AI infra, energy, etc.) |
| Day trading | Avoid | No edge vs. market makers |

**Discomfort:** Macro tail risk can destroy correct fundamental thesis.
**Natural hedges:** Commodity positions can hedge geopolitical prediction market bets.

---

### Bonds 🔶 Narrow
| Duration | Comfort | Use Case |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Short bills | High | Cash equivalent / yield while deploying elsewhere |
| 2–5yr | Medium | Clear rate cycle inflections (Fed regime changes) |
| 10yr+ | Low | Too sensitive to inflation surprise; poor asymmetry |
| HY/Credit | Avoid | Requires credit infrastructure not yet built |

**Honest assessment:** Least analytical edge here. Rate prediction is hard; upside asymmetry is poor.

---

### Commodities 🟡 Event-driven only
| Duration | Comfort | Use Case |
|----------|---------|----------|
| 1–8 weeks | Medium | Geopolitical event plays (Iran strike → crude spike) |
| 3–12 months | Low | Macro hedge only (gold/silver) |
| Softs | Avoid | No weather/crop modelling infrastructure |

**Key insight:** Commodity positions can naturally hedge prediction market bets.
Example: Long crude oil hedges an Iran NO bet — asymmetric protection.

---

### Crypto 🟡 Momentum-driven

| 1–6 months | Low | Violent sentiment cycles; hard to edge |
